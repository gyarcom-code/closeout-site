Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

CloseOut collects nothing. It has no account, no sign-up, and no server. The app contains no networking code at all — it cannot send your records anywhere, because there is nowhere to send them.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter — your jobs, your shifts, your hours, tips, sales and tip-out — is stored on your device.

If you are signed in to iCloud, those records are also synced through your own private iCloud database, so they appear on your other devices. That database belongs to your Apple ID and counts against your iCloud storage. The developer of CloseOut cannot see it, list it, or reach it in any way.

Once a week, if you have records and iCloud Drive is available, the app writes a plain readable CSV backup to its folder in your own iCloud Drive. It is your file, in your account. You can open, copy or delete it from the Files app.

What the app never does

  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting services.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No account, no email address, no password, no profile.
  • No network requests. The app contains no HTTP client and contacts no server — not ours, not anyone's.

Purchases

CloseOut Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Payment details never pass through the app, and the developer never sees them. Apple provides the developer with aggregated, anonymised sales reports, which do not identify individual buyers.

Whether you hold the purchase is determined by asking Apple's own StoreKit each time. Nothing about your purchase is stored by the app or sent anywhere.

Face ID and Touch ID

The optional app lock uses the system's own authentication. Biometric data never leaves your device and is never made available to the app — CloseOut only learns whether the system said yes or no. The lock covers the screen; it does not encrypt your records, and it can never lock you out of them.

Data you export

CSV files, income proof PDFs and daily tip records are produced on your device and handed to the system share sheet. Where they go from there — email, Files, another app — is your choice, and what happens to them afterwards is outside the app's control and this policy.

Children

CloseOut is a tool for people doing paid work in the United States. It is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Your records are yours

  • Export everything to CSV at any time, free, from Settings → Your data. There is no purchase requirement and no limit.
  • Erase everything from the same screen. The confirmation names the exact number of shifts and jobs, and you can choose separately whether to remove the synced copies from your iCloud account.
  • Delete the app and the local records go with it. Anything synced remains in your own iCloud until you erase it there.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the previous versions remain in the public repository's history.
