What you walked out with, worked out properly.

A shift log for servers, bartenders, bussers and runners in the United States. Enter your hours, tips and sales at the end of a shift. CloseOut works out what you owe each coworker, what you keep, and what you actually took home.

It also checks your pay

In most states an employer may pay a tipped worker below the ordinary minimum wage, provided tips make up the difference across the workweek. When they do not, the employer owes the shortfall — and almost nobody checks.

CloseOut checks. Every pay week, against your state's own figures, with the working shown.

And your taxes

Tips you hand to coworkers are not your income, and the IRS says to report only what you receive and keep. CloseOut computes your tip base net of tip-out, keeps service charges out of it where they belong, and tracks your qualified tips against the federal deduction cap through the year — while there is still time to do something about it.
